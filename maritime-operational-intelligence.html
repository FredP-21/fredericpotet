Case study 01 · Environment & Compliance

Environmental compliance at sea

Location-aware fuel changeover and regulatory evidence

Operational challenge

Commercial vessels must meet different fuel-emission requirements when approaching and entering regulated Emission Control Areas (ECAs). A late or missed fuel changeover can create compliance exposure, operational disruption and financial penalties. Captains and shore-based teams need consistent, timely guidance and a reliable record of events during the voyage.

From vessel data to operational action

Multi-source inputs

AIS positioning and movement; onboard fuel-system data; ECA boundaries and compliance rules.

Context-aware logic

Identify approaching regulated zones, fuel-changeover requirements and possible delays.

Decision support

Timely prompts to captain and headquarters; fuel-use and changeover records for reporting.

Capabilities and customer value

  • Proactive guidance: alert the captain when a required fuel changeover must be completed before entering a regulated zone.
  • Shore-side visibility: give headquarters a consistent view of fleet fuel-changeover status and potential compliance exceptions.
  • Traceability: capture fuel usage and changeover events to support voyage-level regulatory records and reporting.

Product leadership contribution

Translated maritime regulatory and operational needs into product capabilities for shipboard and shore-based users, working with engineering to integrate positioning, onboard system signals and compliance workflows into a scalable maritime software platform.

Case study 02 · Safety & Edge AI

Predictive maritime safety

Onboard AI for real-time vessel risk and condition intelligence

Operational challenge

At sea, operational risk and equipment deterioration vary with vessel movement, speed, route, sea state and weather. Retrospective reporting and fixed maintenance schedules offer limited visibility into each voyage's conditions. Crews and fleet operators need actionable intelligence close to where decisions are made.

From vessel telemetry to risk intelligence

Onboard inputs

NMEA and vessel-system signals; ship motion, speed, position, sea state and weather forecasts.

Edge AI processing

Models running onboard estimate operational severity, risk, wear and performance degradation.

Operator intelligence

Risk indications for captain and headquarters; maintenance insights; cumulative voyage exposure.

Capabilities and customer value

  • Real-time safety awareness: provide crews and headquarters with an informed picture of operating risk and changing voyage conditions.
  • Condition-informed maintenance: identify deterioration patterns and emerging issues to guide maintenance planning and records.
  • Fleet intelligence: aggregate incidents, severity and cumulative voyage exposure to support fleet-wide risk management.

Product leadership contribution

Helped lead the transition from laboratory-stage maritime data and AI technology toward operational deployment across geographically dispersed fleets: shaping product vision and priorities, translating operator needs into useful outputs, and working closely with engineering on execution and scale.
