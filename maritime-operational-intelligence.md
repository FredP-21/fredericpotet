# Maritime Operational Intelligence

**Selected product experience: environmental compliance and predictive maritime safety**

Two high-level case studies of transforming vessel data and onboard intelligence into operational decision support.

## Case Study 1: Environmental compliance at sea

*Location-aware fuel changeover and regulatory evidence*

### Operational challenge

Commercial vessels must meet different fuel-emission requirements when approaching regulated Emission Control Areas (ECAs). Delayed or missed fuel changeover can create compliance exposure, operational disruption and penalties. Captains and shore-based teams need timely guidance and a reliable voyage record.

### From vessel data to operational action

1. **Multi-source inputs:** AIS position and movement; onboard fuel-system data; ECA boundaries and compliance rules.
2. **Context-aware logic:** Identify approaching controlled zones, fuel-changeover requirements and possible delays.
3. **Decision support:** Timely prompts to captain and headquarters; fuel-use and changeover records for reporting.

### Capabilities and customer value

- **Proactive guidance:** Alert captains when a required fuel changeover must be completed before entering a regulated zone.
- **Shore-side visibility:** Provide headquarters with fuel-changeover status and compliance exceptions across the fleet.
- **Traceability:** Capture fuel usage and changeover events for voyage-level compliance records.

### Product leadership contribution

Translated maritime regulatory and operational needs into shipboard and shore-based product capabilities, working with engineering to integrate positioning, onboard signals and compliance workflows into a scalable maritime software platform.

## Case Study 2: Predictive maritime safety

*Onboard AI for real-time vessel risk and condition intelligence*

### Operational challenge

Operational risk and equipment deterioration at sea vary with vessel motion, speed, route, sea state and weather. Retrospective reports and fixed maintenance schedules offer limited visibility into voyage conditions. Crews and fleet operators need actionable risk intelligence near the point of decision.

### From vessel telemetry to risk intelligence

1. **Onboard inputs:** NMEA and vessel-system signals; ship motion, speed, position, sea state and weather forecasts.
2. **Edge AI processing:** Models running onboard estimate risk, operational severity, wear and performance degradation.
3. **Operator intelligence:** Risk indications for captain and headquarters; maintenance insights; cumulative voyage exposure.

### Capabilities and customer value

- **Real-time safety awareness:** Continuously informed picture of vessel operating risk and changing conditions.
- **Condition-informed maintenance:** Identify deterioration patterns and emerging issues to prioritise maintenance and update records.
- **Fleet intelligence:** Aggregate incidents, severity and cumulative exposure to help manage fleet-wide operational risk.

### Product leadership contribution

Helped lead the transition from laboratory-stage maritime data and AI technology toward deployment across geographically dispersed fleets: shaping product vision and priorities, translating operator needs into useful outputs and working closely with engineering on execution and scale.
