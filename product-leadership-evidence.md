# Product Leadership — Evidence & Impact

**Selected experience across AI, data platforms, edge computing and connected systems**

## 01. Product scale & adoption

**600 devices**

Owned internationally deployed maritime edge-computing products spanning approximately 600 connected devices. Each device served an average of three daily active users, across five user personas including marine crews and data scientists — approximately 1,800 daily user-device instances across the fleet (not a count of distinct individuals). Led platform capability, reliability and evolution in a distributed operational environment.

## 02. Experimentation & iterative delivery

**Twice-weekly reviews**

Led iterative co-development of an AI agent on NBCU's enterprise intelligence layer, aligning objectives with the VP of Data Enablement. Established twice-weekly user reviews and prototype testing during peak development. Early negative feedback drove changes to design, prompts and workflows before significant engineering investment. This was prototype-led validation, not a high-volume A/B testing programme.

## 03. Measurable impact & product governance

**~40% → 70%+**

Personally developed an AI agent scanning data-quality rule coverage across GCP environments, identifying gaps against six established quality dimensions and proposing missing controls for deployment through GitHub CI/CD. Rule coverage increased from approximately 40% to over 70% in four weeks. Service KPIs and operational signals also informed executive reporting, investment choices and lifecycle roadmaps.

## 04. Live & behavioural data products

**Time-sensitive intelligence**

Led platforms transforming live signals into time-sensitive decisions. Maritime edge processing of vessel telemetry supported performance monitoring and predictive-maintenance AI. At NBCU, low-latency streaming-app clickstream and browsing signals enabled ML services for near-real-time content personalisation. Product ownership centred on dependable event processing, availability and downstream AI enablement.

## 05. Cross-functional ML platform delivery

**Shared feature store**

Worked with data scientists, ML engineers, data engineers and analysts to deliver enterprise AI/data services. At NBCU, led shared feature-store capabilities for model training and real-time inference serving, aligned with data-science roadmaps. Also delivered data quality, governance, catalogue, self-service and agentic workflow capabilities.

## 06. Applied AI & agentic automation

**Product-led and hands-on**

Used Microsoft Copilot across Teams and Microsoft 365 for synthesis, communication and planning; Cursor and Augment for AI-assisted development; and a LangGraph-based enterprise intelligence layer for operational agents. Personally developed the GCP data-quality coverage agent and helped shape an agentic portfolio for data exploration, enablement, analysis and workflow automation.
