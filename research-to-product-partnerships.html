Product leadership case study · Vix Technology · 2020–2021

Building an AI/ML innovation and partner ecosystem

Creating professional services and a Labs activity to complement global Data & AI platform strategy

Business and product context

At Vix Technology, a global provider of public-transport ticketing and mobility platforms, I worked on developing a global Data & AI platform proposition. This included connecting technology capabilities, partners and commercial models across an international mobility ecosystem.

What I established

Professional services

Created a professional-services activity to support the emerging data and AI product proposition.

AI/ML Labs activity

Created a Labs initiative engaging universities across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe.

Technology ecosystem

Engaged leading data-service providers alongside the academic collaboration activity.

Product strategy and commercialisation contribution

  • Developed a global Data & AI platform strategy with executive sponsorship, including feature propositions and partner-ecosystem considerations.
  • Prepared business cases, product propositions and roadmaps, connecting technical opportunities with customer and business priorities.
  • Developed tiered SaaS commercial models and worked on cloud data-platform cost optimisation.
  • Supported RFI/RFP processes and sales collateral; presented propositions to executive stakeholders including CxO, COO and CPO audiences.

Why this experience transfers to deep-tech product leadership

It demonstrates experience initiating and coordinating academic and industry relationships alongside the product and commercial work needed to bring emerging technology into enterprise propositions. That combination is relevant to research-intensive companies, including quantum computing and advanced AI businesses, without implying previous quantum computing product delivery.
